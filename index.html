How pricing labels work

100% Free means the listed core functionality does not require a paid subscription or timed trial. External sources can still impose quotas, policies, or separate costs. Freemium / Paid tier means a free option exists but some functions, allowances, or licenses require payment.

Pricing and policies can change. Each listing includes source links and an import date. This catalog contains 880 source listings after filtering supplied data. Tool links and prices are not independently checked in every case. Search ranking prioritizes relevance, then an editorial quality heuristic. Category ranking uses that heuristic; editor's picks are not uptime or maintenance guarantees.
